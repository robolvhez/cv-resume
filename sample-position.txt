Who We Are & What We Do 

At Helix, our mission is simple: to help everyone improve their lives through their DNA. Ready to make a real-world impact with your skills? At Helix, we're transforming healthcare by making genomics a standard of care. We partner with health systems and life science companies to accelerate the integration of genomic data into clinical practice. Join us in building a future where healthcare is personalized, proactive, and powered by genomics. 

Your Career Path & Development

We are committed to your long-term growth. We invest in you through:
AI-Native Engineering: We've standardized on a strong, well-integrated set of agentic coding tools, so you spend your time directing agents that plan, write, and test code rather than evaluating a dozen options yourself.
Continuous Learning: Sharpen your skills with full access to the entire Udemy learning database.
Internal Mentorship: Learn from experts across our global team through tech demos, pair programming, and collaborative projects.
Clear Advancement: We provide a framework for you to grow from one role to the next, with clear expectations and support from your manager.
Travel: Opportunities to travel to the US for collaboration with our global teams.

What is special about these roles?

At Helix, our engineering teams build the systems that make genomic medicine real - from the lab bench to the patient portal to the research platform. We're hiring at the Senior and Senior II levels across four teams. Depending on your background and interests, you may be matched to one of the following:

Experience (Patient & Provider) - Builds the product experiences that connect patients, health systems, and healthcare providers to genomic insights. The Patient team (PHP) owns the HRN enrollment flow, results delivery, and accessioning integrations. The Provider team (PRO) simplifies the ordering, management, and billing of clinical genetic tests.
Lab Systems (LABS) - Owns the laboratory-facing services that keeps Helix’s sequencing & supply chain operations running. You’ll build and maintain scalable platforms and fullstack software that support the lab and integrates with analysis pipelines: lab workflows, instrument automations, sample accessioning, inventory management and distribution.
Data, Operations & Governance (DOG) - Owns how Helix accesses, models, and governs its data. This team builds the backend services, APIs, pipelines and infrastructure behind it, and owns the core data model that Strategy and Operations teams build on. Governance and privacy are a core mandate and the team owns the services that track which data exists, who can use it, and how it's accessed.
Genomics Platform (GENP) - Builds the infrastructure, pipelines, and applications that process terabytes of genomic data weekly. Supports exome sequencing, confirmation testing, bioinformatics QC, and return-of-results workflows.

As a Senior Software Engineer, you will:

Lead projects from scoping through implementation to successful outcomes — not just output, but real impact for the business and users.
Delegate implementation to AI agents with precise, unambiguous instructions, then critically evaluate output for correctness and production-readiness using evals and other quality checks, maintaining audit-ready compliance throughout. 
Explore and champion new AI tools, frameworks, and workflows, including orchestrating multiple AI agents across a pipeline; document pros/cons, best practices, and pitfalls; and create reusable prompt templates or context frameworks that elevate the entire team's AI fluency.
Design and implement software systems for Helix's products and services, delivering well-tested, performant, and reliable solutions that consider the full lifecycle of a project (including on-call).
Take ownership of services and significant areas of the codebase, developing deep domain expertise and serving as a go-to technical resource for your team.
Collaborate cross-functionally with product managers, designers, user experience researchers, scientists, and business leaders to drive meaningful outcomes.
Proactively identify and address technical debt before it becomes a long-term problem, and encourage and enable your team to do the same.
Mentor engineers within your team and contribute to improving engineering practices, with growing influence beyond your immediate team.
Contribute to setting technical standards and participate in decision-making that affects teams beyond your direct sphere of influence.

About you:

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
7+ years of equivalent professional experience as a Software Engineer.
Proficiency with distributed systems built on cloud infrastructure - AWS, GCP, Azure, or similar.
Strong engineering expertise with proficiency in an established language - backend (Go, Java, Python, Scala, Rust) or full-stack (TypeScript/React + a backend language)
Daily, hands-on use of AI coding harnesses as a core part of your development workflow.
Demonstrated ability to delegate implementation work to AI agents. This means decomposing problems, writing clear and precise instructions, and iterating on prompts to get production-quality results.
Solid understanding of software engineering best practices including testing, code review, CI/CD, and observability.
Demonstrated ability to take a nebulous project, scope it, determine a solution and course of action, and implement and launch it with minimal external guidance.
A strong product engineering mindset with a clear focus on customer and business outcomes.
Experience mentoring other engineers and a track record of elevating the practices of those around you.
Familiarity with infrastructure-as-code tooling/frameworks (e.g., Terraform, CloudFormation, AWS CDK).
Familiarity with database design, data manipulation, and data quality techniques.
Adaptable in a fast-paced startup environment where priorities may change quickly and frequently.
Demonstrated willingness to learn new domains (e.g., genomics, healthcare) and associated technologies.
Fluent in English, both written and spoken.

Pluses:

Familiarity with regulated software systems in any industry (e.g., HIPAA, ISO, FDA).
Familiarity and experience developing and architecting event-driven architectures.
Experience with AWS technologies and serverless infrastructure.
Experience in the life sciences/pharmaceutical industry.

What To Expect During Your Onboarding Week:
We want to make sure your first week is spent learning the foundations. You will be invited to live orientation sessions and spend time with your hiring manager developing your individual roadmap to success. This experience will include sessions that cover your benefits, security & privacy training, an introduction to the OKR framework used at Helix and our communication tools and norms. 

What To Expect During Your First 90 days:
First 5 days: You’ll spend time learning the Helix way, completing training and onboarding for your roles, and getting introduced to your team and relevant stakeholders. You’ll also gain a deeper understanding of our customers, our products, the impact we make in the lives of our communities, and how to thrive at Helix through participation in Helix U.  

Day 6 - 30: You’ll spend time contributing to projects, deeply familiarizing yourself with team and company processes, and developing a deeper understanding of Helix’s products, services and capabilities. 

Day 31 - 90: You’ll build your OKRs with your manager, start to take ownership of projects and initiatives on your team, and begin to demonstrate your impact on the Helix mission.
